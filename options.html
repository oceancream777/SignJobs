HIRING POST SKIMMER

Less opening. More knowing.

A compact reading layer for the hiring posts you browse yourself.

Your reading mode

Rules tag every available post. Auto mode interprets visible posts one at a time. Each explanation uses at most two lines; inferred work is labelled Likely. Backend requests and automated comments peeking are not included.

Optional on-device model

The model is optional and experimental until benchmarked on your M1. Requires WebGPU with shader-f16. Each request has a 90-second cold-load/generation limit; fallback remains available. The worker unloads after 60 idle seconds. The 1.44 GB download is optional and does not measure peak RAM use.

Your categories

One category per line: Category | synonym, synonym. Other and Not hiring are always available.

Explicit author cues

One phrase per line. “Link below” is intentionally omitted because it does not necessarily mean comments. Negated phrases never create a positive instruction.

Your data stays yours

We read text and links already present in LinkedIn content-search posts, including CSS-clamped text, plus comments you have already opened. Missing text stays missing. We never expand, search, scroll, load comments, or contact LinkedIn servers.

Post bodies are never saved. Settings and explicitly Saved/Applied items remain in this browser. A saved item contains only its ID, role title, company, application/post links, status and timestamp. Export contains these fields only.

LinkedIn restricts extensions that modify or scrape its pages. Passive design reduces automation, but does not guarantee account safety or permission under LinkedIn’s terms.
